Book a wash or check coverage

Send your area, preferred day and whether you want a single wash or monthly plan. We currently serve Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October. We will confirm the appointment and service details together on WhatsApp.

Change an appointment

Send the name and appointment details used for your booking, followed by the change you need. Contact us as soon as you can so we can check available alternatives. A change is confirmed once we agree it with you.

Something needs our attention

Tell us which visit your concern relates to and what happened. Add relevant photos if they help explain the issue. Please avoid sharing unrelated personal or payment information. We will review the details with you.

Privacy requests

Use the same WhatsApp number for questions about your information, access or correction requests, deletion requests, or privacy concerns. Start your message with “Privacy request” so the purpose is clear.
