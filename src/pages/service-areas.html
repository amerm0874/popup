Coverage questions.

Do you cover all of Cairo?+

Our currently stated service areas are Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October. For other locations, ask the team first; coverage is not guaranteed outside these areas.

Can I book a same-day visit?+

Send your location and preferred time on WhatsApp. The team checks availability and confirms an appointment; same-day service is not guaranteed.

Does a monthly plan confirm every appointment automatically?+

No. We coordinate the visit schedule with you. You can choose 2, 4 or 8 washes per month and agree the appointments with the team.
