Everything else? Just ask us.

Where do you operate?

We currently come to homes in Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October City. Send your location to confirm availability.

How do I book?

Choose a wash or subscription and send us a WhatsApp enquiry. We confirm your location, service details and appointment with you.

What happens after the first month?

The 4-wash plan renews at EGP 1,400 per month, and the 8-wash plan at EGP 2,500. The 2-wash plan is EGP 750 per month.

What does the wash include?

A complete interior, exterior and trunk wash using high-quality cleaning products.

Can I change my appointment?

Message us to request a change. We will confirm the available alternatives and any applicable conditions.
