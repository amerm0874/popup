Our service

Pop Up provides mobile car washing at your location in Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October City. The wash includes the interior, exterior and trunk, using high-quality cleaning products. Coverage, site access and any particular requirements are confirmed before the visit.

How a booking is confirmed

This website lets you explore services and start a WhatsApp enquiry. Clicking a booking link does not reserve an appointment or take payment. Your booking is confirmed through our conversation after we agree on availability, location, service, price and payment arrangements.

Please provide accurate contact and location details and tell us about access restrictions or specific concerns about the car before the visit. We will agree any necessary arrangements with you.

Prices and monthly plans

A single wash costs EGP 450. The 2-wash monthly plan costs EGP 750. The 4-wash monthly plan costs EGP 1,000 for the first month, then EGP 1,400 per month. The 8-wash monthly plan costs EGP 1,800 for the first month, then EGP 2,500 per month.

We confirm the total price and relevant plan details before you commit. This website does not automatically renew subscriptions or charge you. Ask us to confirm scheduling, the plan period and treatment of unused washes before starting a monthly plan.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

If you need to change or cancel a visit, contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible. Any applicable change, cancellation or refund terms will be explained before you commit to the booking.

If we need to change an appointment, we will contact you to agree the next step. Nothing here removes any refund or other rights you have under applicable consumer law.

Service concerns

Tell us on WhatsApp if something about your wash is not right. Include the appointment details and a description or relevant photos so we can review the issue and agree an appropriate response. These terms do not exclude responsibilities or consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Website information

Illustrative images are identified on the website and are not evidence of a specific team member or vehicle. Prices and availability are confirmed when booking. Changes to these website terms do not change an already agreed booking without an appropriate agreement with you.
